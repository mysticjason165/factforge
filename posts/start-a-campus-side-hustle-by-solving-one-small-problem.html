Start a Campus Side Hustle by Solving One Small Problem

FactForge · 1 min read

A campus side hustle does not need an impressive name. It needs a problem you can solve reliably. Think about tasks students already struggle to fit into their week: formatting a presentation, organizing an event signup, or learning a difficult class concept.

Choose one service and describe its limits. A slide-formatting offer might cover ten slides, one revision, and a two-day turnaround. Clear limits let someone understand what they are buying and help you estimate the work before accepting it.

Test the offer with a small, permission-based announcement where campus rules allow it. Track time, expenses, and feedback rather than treating the first payment as proof of a business. Protect your class schedule, and never offer to complete graded work for someone else. A useful service should support student life, including your own.

Compare Your Work with a Useful Reference, Not Everyone

FactForge · 1 min read

Comparison becomes difficult to use when the reference keeps changing. One person has more views, another has better equipment, and someone else appears further ahead in life. Those separate observations do not form a fair standard for your own next step.

Choose a comparison with a purpose. If you want clearer writing, inspect how one good article introduces its subject. If you want better videos, study its sound or pacing rather than the creator's entire lifestyle. Turn the observation into a specific skill you can practice.

Also compare your current work with an earlier version of your own. Identify what improved and what still needs attention. You can admire someone without adopting their timeline. A useful comparison produces a possible action. When it only produces a broad feeling of inadequacy, it may be time to change what you are measuring.

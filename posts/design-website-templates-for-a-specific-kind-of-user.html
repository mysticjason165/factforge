Design Website Templates for a Specific Kind of User

FactForge · 1 min read

A website template becomes easier to design when you know who will fill it in. A tutor needs different sections from a photographer. Choosing one audience lets you make useful decisions about layout before adding decorative details.

Build with realistic sample content and label it clearly. Test long names, missing images, and narrow screens. Include instructions for changing colors, replacing pictures, and adding a page. The customer should not need to reverse-engineer your files to update a contact detail.

Remove features that look interactive but do nothing. Check that fonts and images can be redistributed under the terms you intend. Show the actual template in the sales preview. A good template offers a strong starting structure while leaving room for the buyer's own content; it should not become a fragile design that breaks after one sentence changes.

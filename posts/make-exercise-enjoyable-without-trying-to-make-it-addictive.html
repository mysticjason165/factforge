Make Exercise Enjoyable Without Trying to Make It Addictive

FactForge · 1 min read

An activity is easier to consider repeating when something about the experience appeals to you. That might be the setting, the movement, or the company. You do not need to turn exercise into an obsession to give it a place in your life.

Try a suitable activity and pay attention to the practical experience. Was the location convenient? Did you feel comfortable there? Would a different time or format improve it? These questions can be more useful than assuming you lack discipline because you dislike one particular workout.

Keep room for rest and other interests. Enjoyment should support a balanced routine rather than pressure you to continue regardless of circumstances. You can prefer an ordinary walk over a trendy class. The aim is to find a form of movement you can approach willingly and sensibly, not a habit that makes you feel unable to stop.

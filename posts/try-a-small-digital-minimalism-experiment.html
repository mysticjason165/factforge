Try a Small Digital-Minimalism Experiment

FactForge · 1 min read

A technology break does not have to remove every useful tool. Start by separating necessary activities from the habits you want to examine. Work messages, navigation, and a conversation with a friend are different from opening a feed automatically.

Choose one optional behavior to limit for a defined period. Decide what will replace it, how people can reach you when needed, and what exceptions make sense. Removing an app without planning for the empty time can leave you simply finding another way to scroll.

At the end, review what you missed and what you did not. Keep the changes that gave you something valuable, such as uninterrupted reading or a calmer meal. This approach follows the focus manuscript's emphasis on deliberate use. The purpose is to learn about your habits, not to win a contest for spending the least time online.

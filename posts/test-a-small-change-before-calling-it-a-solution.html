Test a Small Change Before Calling It a Solution

FactForge · 1 min read

A change that sounds sensible can still fail in practice. Define the problem in observable terms before trying to solve it. Saying files take too long to locate is more testable than saying the whole system is terrible.

Choose one adjustment and decide what you will observe. You might test a naming convention on one folder before reorganizing an entire archive. Keep the original available so the experiment is reversible. Change fewer things at once when that makes the result easier to interpret.

Review what happened, including inconveniences you did not predict. A result from one situation may not apply everywhere. Keep, revise, or abandon the change based on what you learned. The useful habit is making claims about improvements answerable to experience, rather than treating the first promising idea as a finished solution.

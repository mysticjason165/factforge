Understand Moderate Exercise Without Chasing a Perfect Zone

FactForge · 1 min read

Exercise intensity is relative to the person doing it. A pace that feels easy to one person may feel demanding to another. The CDC's talk test offers a simple description: during moderate activity, you can generally talk but not sing.

Use that as a broad guide rather than a challenge to push harder. Start with an activity suited to your abilities and notice how the effort feels. A watch's zone label is not necessary for every ordinary walk, and a general rule cannot account for every health condition.

Choose a pace and duration you can approach sensibly, building gradually when appropriate. Stop for concerning symptoms and seek help when needed. The point of understanding intensity is to make activity easier to interpret. It should not turn a manageable routine into a contest to maintain an exact number at all costs.

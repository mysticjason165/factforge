Ask What a Product Claim Leaves Out

FactForge · 1 min read

A product description can emphasize a true detail while leaving the practical question unanswered. A device may be faster in one task without being better for the task you need. Read the comparison as carefully as the headline.

Ask faster than what, tested how, and under which conditions. Check whether extra equipment, fees, or setup are needed for the advertised result. Examples chosen by a seller may show possibilities without describing a typical experience.

Compare the claim with your requirements before getting drawn into additional features. Keep questions in a short list and look for direct answers. Missing information is not proof of deception, but it is a reason to avoid filling the gap with your most favorable assumption. A useful purchase decision connects the advertised capability with something you will actually use.

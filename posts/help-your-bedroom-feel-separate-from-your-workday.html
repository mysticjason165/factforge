Help Your Bedroom Feel Separate from Your Workday

FactForge · 1 min read

When the bedroom is also an office, work can remain visually present long after you stop. You may not be able to change rooms, but you can create a clearer ending to the work period.

Close the work materials, put loose papers in a folder, and write tomorrow's first step before leaving the desk. If possible, move the laptop out of direct sight. The NHLBI's sleep guidance supports a quiet, cool, dark sleep setting; choose practical changes suited to your home rather than buying an entire new setup.

Agree on reasonable shared-room arrangements when other people are involved. Keep the routine simple enough to repeat on busy nights. A tidier boundary does not guarantee sleep, but it can prevent unfinished work from remaining the center of the room. Persistent sleep difficulties deserve more attention than another round of decorating.

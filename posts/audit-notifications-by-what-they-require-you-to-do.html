Audit Notifications by What They Require You to Do

FactForge · 1 min read

A notification can announce anything from a real appointment change to a suggestion that you open an app. Treating them all as equally urgent lets software choose too much of the day's order.

Review one app at a time. Ask whether each alert requires timely action, provides useful information later, or mainly invites checking. Keep necessary alerts and reduce the rest where the settings allow. Test the arrangement so important contacts can still reach you when appropriate.

Review the result after a few days instead of switching everything off impulsively. Some responsibilities require availability; others do not. The focus manuscript's useful distinction is that receiving information does not automatically create an obligation to respond immediately. A notification audit puts that decision back into a deliberate routine rather than leaving every sound or banner to make it for you.

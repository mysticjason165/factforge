Make a Simple Motion Graphic for a Local Business

FactForge · 1 min read

A moving graphic should make a message easier to notice and understand. If every element spins or flashes, the viewer may remember the movement and miss the event date. Start with the single action or piece of information the business wants to communicate.

Create a readable still layout before animating it. Then add movement that supports the hierarchy, such as bringing in the headline before the details. Check the design at phone size and make sure essential information remains on screen long enough to read.

Confirm names, dates, prices, and logo permissions with the client before delivery. Export the agreed sizes and include a still version when useful. Keep the project file organized for future corrections. The best first project is one you can deliver clearly and revise reliably, rather than an ambitious effect that overwhelms the original message.

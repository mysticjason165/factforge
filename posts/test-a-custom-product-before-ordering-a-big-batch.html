Test a Custom Product Before Ordering a Big Batch

FactForge · 1 min read

A club shirt or event sticker can look exciting on a screen and still attract few buyers. A design is an idea; demand is a separate question. Mixing them up can leave you with a box of products and less money for the next attempt.

Create a clear mockup and ask the intended group about size, design, price, and timing. A compliment is useful feedback, but it is not an order. Before collecting payment, confirm production costs, delivery expectations, permissions for logos, and how you would handle a cancellation.

Begin with a small, manageable run when practical. Inspect a sample for print quality and fit rather than trusting the mockup alone. Keep a record of what people requested and what you actually delivered. The goal of the first version is to learn whether you can fulfill a real need without taking on more inventory than you can manage.

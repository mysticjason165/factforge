Build a Plant-Based Meal Around More Than a Side Salad

FactForge · 1 min read

A plant-based meal can have a clear center: lentils, beans, tofu, or another food you enjoy. Starting there can make the meal easier to plan than removing meat from a familiar plate and leaving the rest unchanged.

Add vegetables and a grain or other suitable accompaniment, then choose seasoning that makes the dish appealing. A bean bowl and a tofu stir-fry are simple examples rather than fixed prescriptions. Harvard's Healthy Eating Plate lists beans and nuts among protein options and encourages variety across the meal.

Consider your overall diet and individual needs, especially when excluding entire food groups. Professional nutrition advice can help with more specific planning. For the next grocery trip, choose one recipe you can realistically cook rather than replacing every meal at once. A useful change is one that remains practical when the excitement of trying something new has passed.

Change the Setup Before Blaming Your Willpower

FactForge · 1 min read

A habit can become harder when the environment keeps inviting its opposite. If you want to read but the phone is open beside the book, each page begins with another choice. The focus manuscript encourages making the intended activity easier to approach.

Change one part of the setup. Put the book where you sit, close the unrelated app, or leave the next document ready. Keep necessary tools and communication available in a way that fits your responsibilities. The change should reduce friction rather than create a new elaborate ritual.

Try it and observe what happens. If the obstacle remains, revise the setup or the task instead of turning the result into a character judgment. Environment is not the only influence on behavior, but it is often a practical place to experiment because the change can be small and reversible.

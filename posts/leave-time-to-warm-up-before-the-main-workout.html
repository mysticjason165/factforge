Leave Time to Warm Up Before the Main Workout

FactForge · 1 min read

A workout plan can be unrealistic if it counts only the hardest part. Getting ready and easing into activity also take time. The NHS provides a warm-up routine as an example of preparing before exercise.

Choose preparation suited to the activity and your abilities. Begin gently rather than rushing from sitting still into your most demanding effort. Follow appropriate instruction, and do not force a movement that causes pain. A generic sequence may need adjustment for individual limitations.

Include the warm-up when deciding how much time the session needs. If the available window is too short, modify the plan rather than treating preparation as disposable. A warm-up is not a guarantee against injury, but it deserves a place in the routine. The first few minutes should help you approach the session deliberately, not become another opportunity to compete.

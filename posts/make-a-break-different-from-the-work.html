Make a Break Different from the Work

FactForge · 1 min read

If your work happens on a screen, switching to another feed may not feel like much of a change. A break can be an opportunity to leave the task briefly rather than replace it with a new stream of decisions.

Try a comfortable change of position, a short walk if accessible, or a moment away from the desk. Pick an option that fits your surroundings and abilities. The CDC includes movement and time to unwind among ways to manage stress; no single break needs to become a complete wellness routine.

Before stepping away, write one sentence about where to resume. This can make returning less confusing. Notice whether the break leaves you more comfortable or simply runs longer than intended. Adjust the length and activity to your situation. Breaks support the day best when they are possible to take, not when they require elaborate preparation.

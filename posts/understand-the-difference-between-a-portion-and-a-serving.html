Understand the Difference Between a Portion and a Serving

FactForge · 1 min read

A serving on a food label and the portion you choose to eat are not necessarily the same amount. NIDDK explains this distinction because it affects how you interpret the information on a package.

Before using the nutrition numbers, check the serving size and the number of servings in the container. Then compare that with what you actually plan to eat. This is an information step, not a command that everyone must eat the listed amount.

Your needs depend on more than the package, including the rest of the meal and your individual situation. Avoid turning label reading into a rigid rule or a reason to ignore hunger. Use it when it helps you make an informed choice. Understanding what a number refers to is more useful than treating the number itself as a judgment about whether a food is allowed.

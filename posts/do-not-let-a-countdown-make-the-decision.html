Do Not Let a Countdown Make the Decision

FactForge · 1 min read

A deadline can be real, but urgency does not prove an offer is suitable. When a message insists that you act immediately, separate the time limit from the reasons you would accept the offer at all.

Write down what you know about the product, cost, seller, and terms. Identify what remains unverified. If the missing information is important, a countdown does not make it less important. You can decline a decision that cannot be checked in the time provided.

For ordinary purchases, compare the offer with your original need and budget rather than the feeling of missing out. You may occasionally pass on something legitimate. That is still different from being obligated to act because someone else set the clock. A useful decision has reasons that remain understandable after the urgency has faded.

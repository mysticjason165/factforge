Adapt an Older Article for a Short Video

FactForge · 1 min read

An article and a short video ask for different kinds of attention. A paragraph that works on a page may sound stiff when read aloud. Begin by choosing one point from the article rather than trying to compress every section.

Write a spoken explanation with a concrete opening example. Show a visual only when it helps someone follow the idea. Keep enough context to avoid turning a careful argument into a misleading claim. If a statement depends on an exception, preserve that exception even when the format is short.

Read the script aloud before recording and remove phrases you would never naturally say. Point viewers to the original article for details and sources. Repurposing works best as adaptation: the new format should offer a complete small lesson while giving interested people a path to the fuller discussion.

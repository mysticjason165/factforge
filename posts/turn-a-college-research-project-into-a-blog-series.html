Turn a College Research Project into a Blog Series

FactForge · 1 min read

A substantial college project often contains more than one useful question. Its background, methods, findings, and limitations can each support a separate explanation. That does not mean copying the same paper into several posts with different headlines.

List the questions a general reader might ask, then choose one per article. Explain specialist terms and use examples that do not require the original class. Preserve the distinction between your interpretation and what a cited source actually found. Check course rules and permissions before sharing interviews, group work, or unpublished material.

Link related posts so readers can go deeper without repeating the entire background every time. Keep the source list from the project available while writing. The series should make the work more accessible, not stretch it into an endless pipeline. A limited collection of clear posts is enough when the questions have been answered.

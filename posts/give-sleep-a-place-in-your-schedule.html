Give Sleep a Place in Your Schedule

FactForge · 1 min read

Sleep can become whatever time remains after work, messages, and entertainment. That makes it difficult to tell whether the schedule is realistic. Begin by treating rest as something the day must make room for, rather than a reward for finishing everything.

The NHLBI recommends seven to nine hours of sleep for adults. Look at your required wake time and work backward to see what an adequate opportunity for sleep would involve. Notice which evening activities regularly expand beyond the time you intended to give them.

Choose one practical adjustment, such as preparing tomorrow's bag earlier or ending an optional task sooner. Do not judge the plan by one night. If sleep problems persist despite having enough opportunity to rest, seek professional guidance. A schedule can remove avoidable obstacles, but it cannot diagnose the reason someone remains tired.

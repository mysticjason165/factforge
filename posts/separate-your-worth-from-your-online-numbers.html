Separate Your Worth from Your Online Numbers

FactForge · 1 min read

A post can receive little attention for reasons you cannot see. Turning that number into a conclusion about your value asks a platform metric to answer a much bigger question than it can handle.

Before publishing, decide what you can judge directly. Was the explanation clear? Did you check the claim? Did you finish something you had been avoiding? Those questions still matter if the post reaches few people. Audience numbers can inform a content decision without becoming a personal score.

Give yourself a reasonable interval before reviewing results, and avoid repeatedly checking a number that has no immediate use. Keep activities and relationships that do not require public measurement. You can care about improving your work while refusing to make each reaction a verdict on who you are. The goal is perspective, not pretending that disappointment never happens.

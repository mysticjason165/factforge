Prepare for Remote Work with One Demonstrable Skill

FactForge · 1 min read

A remote job is still a job with responsibilities, deadlines, and a hiring process. Start by identifying the kind of work you can demonstrate: customer support writing, spreadsheet cleanup, basic design, or another specific skill.

Build a small work sample and explain your process. For spreadsheet cleanup, show how you handled duplicate entries and documented changes using fictional data. Then read actual job descriptions to find gaps between your practice and the role's requirements. Do not claim abilities you have only watched someone else perform.

Verify employers through their official channels. The FTC warns that job scams can involve requests for upfront payments or fake checks. Keep applications and follow-ups in one simple tracker. A repeatable search process will not promise a quick hire, but it can keep you organized and make suspicious offers easier to examine before sharing sensitive information.

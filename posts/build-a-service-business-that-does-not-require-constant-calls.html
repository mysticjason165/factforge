Build a Service Business That Does Not Require Constant Calls

FactForge · 1 min read

Some projects can move forward through a clear brief, written questions, and scheduled updates. This can suit a student or part-time freelancer who cannot be available for calls throughout the day. It requires planning rather than disappearing between deadlines.

Create an intake form that asks for the goal, materials, deadline, and examples. Tell clients when they can expect a response and where feedback should go. Use one place for decisions so changes do not get lost across several conversations. Keep exceptions available for issues that genuinely need a live discussion.

At each milestone, explain what is ready and what input you need next. Written communication should reduce uncertainty, not create a wall between people. Test the process on a small project and revise confusing instructions. A workable schedule depends on shared expectations more than on any particular communication tool.

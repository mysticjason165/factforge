Turn What You Know into a Focused Teaching Offer

FactForge · 1 min read

Knowing a subject and teaching it are related but different skills. A useful teaching offer names the learner, the starting point, and the outcome. Helping a beginner organize a spreadsheet is clearer than offering general success advice.

Design one short lesson around a task the learner can complete. Prepare an example, a practice exercise, and a way to check understanding. Leave room for questions rather than using every minute to talk. Be honest about your experience and stay within subjects you can explain responsibly.

After the lesson, ask what remained confusing and what the learner can now do alone. Use that feedback to adjust the next version. Avoid guaranteed results, particularly in money or health topics. The strongest evidence of a useful lesson is not an impressive description of your knowledge; it is a learner who leaves with a clearer next step.

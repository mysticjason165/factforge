Use Audience Feedback Without Chasing Every Comment

FactForge · 1 min read

Feedback can tell you where a reader gets confused, but it does not automatically tell you what your project should become. One loud request may represent one person's preference. Several independent questions about the same problem deserve closer attention.

Keep a simple feedback log with the question, the page or product involved, and your possible response. Separate broken features from suggestions and personal taste. A broken download needs a different response from a request for another color. This distinction helps you spend time where it has a clear purpose.

Try a small change before rebuilding everything. Explain what changed and invite specific feedback about whether the problem is resolved. Keep your main audience and purpose in view. Listening well means taking people seriously while still making deliberate decisions about which requests fit the work you are trying to do.

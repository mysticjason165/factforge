Choose Paper or a Digital Journal by How You Will Use It

FactForge · 1 min read

The best journal format is not automatically the most advanced one. Paper offers a place without app menus, while a digital document can be easier to search and carry. Each also has privacy and convenience tradeoffs.

Think about when you want to write and what you need afterward. If you only want a short reflection, a plain notebook may be enough. If you need to find past entries by topic, a simple digital system may help. Check how files are stored and shared before recording sensitive details.

Try one format for a manageable period without building a complicated tagging system first. Keep the parts that make writing easier. Switching tools repeatedly can become a substitute for writing anything. The format should support the reflection you want to do, not become the main project in its own right.

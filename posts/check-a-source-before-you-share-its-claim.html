Check a Source Before You Share Its Claim

FactForge · 1 min read

A source deserves more attention when its claim could affect an important decision. Start by finding the original publication rather than relying on a cropped quote or a repost that names no author.

Check who produced it, when it appeared, what evidence it provides, and whether the headline matches the content. Look for relevant expertise without assuming a credential makes every statement correct. If several pages repeat the same original claim, they are not necessarily independent confirmation.

Follow a citation far enough to see whether it supports the sentence using it. When a key detail cannot be verified, describe that limit or wait before sharing. You do not need a complete investigation for every casual observation. But the more consequential the claim, the more useful it is to know where it came from and what the source actually established.

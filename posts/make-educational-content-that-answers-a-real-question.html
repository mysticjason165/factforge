Make Educational Content That Answers a Real Question

FactForge · 1 min read

Before recording an educational video, finish this sentence: after watching, someone should be able to do or understand what? That answer is more useful than a broad topic such as productivity or technology.

Choose one question, show a concrete example, and explain the confusing part. A video about organizing computer files could begin with a messy folder and end with a simple naming system. Viewers can judge the result for themselves. If the lesson includes a factual claim, check its source before turning it into a confident headline.

End when the question is answered. Extra minutes do not automatically add value. Look at the questions viewers still ask and use them to plan the next lesson. Over time, a connected collection of helpful explanations can give an audience a reason to return, although publishing alone does not guarantee income.

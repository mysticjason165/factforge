Make a Digital Guide People Can Navigate

FactForge · 1 min read

A useful guide should help readers find the part they need now. Some will read from the beginning; others will arrive with a specific question. Clear headings and descriptive links can serve both groups without turning the document into a complicated interface.

Organize the guide around tasks. Include a short contents page, define unfamiliar terms where they appear, and place examples beside the relevant explanation. If a worksheet is included, make the instructions visible on that page rather than several pages earlier.

Test every link and check the guide on the devices readers are likely to use. Keep an editable master and record important revisions. A beautiful cover cannot compensate for instructions that send people in circles. Before adding another chapter, ask whether the existing material is complete, accurate, and easy to locate when someone needs it.

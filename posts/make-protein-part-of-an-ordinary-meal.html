Make Protein Part of an Ordinary Meal

FactForge · 1 min read

Protein does not have to arrive in a special product. Beans, tofu, eggs, fish, and other familiar foods can fit into everyday meals. The practical question is how to include a suitable option in the meals you already know how to make.

Begin with one meal that often feels incomplete. You could add beans to a vegetable wrap or tofu to a rice dish. Consider the whole meal rather than letting a single nutrient crowd out variety. Harvard's Healthy Eating Plate includes varied protein choices alongside other food groups.

Choose options that fit your preferences, budget, allergies, and any medical guidance you follow. An exact personal protein target needs more context than a general article can provide. Start by making your meals easier to assemble consistently, rather than buying products because their packaging suggests that ordinary food is no longer enough.

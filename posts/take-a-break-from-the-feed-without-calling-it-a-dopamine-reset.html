Take a Break from the Feed Without Calling It a Dopamine Reset

FactForge · 1 min read

A feed has few natural endings. You can finish one post and immediately receive another, even after forgetting why you opened the app. Calling a pause a chemical reset adds a certainty the idea does not need.

Instead, define the behavior you want to change. Perhaps you want to stop scrolling during breakfast or avoid opening a feed whenever a task becomes difficult. Choose one situation and decide what you will do instead. Put the app out of immediate reach if that makes your plan easier to follow.

Review whether the change gives you time for something you actually value. You do not need to prove that your brain has been reset. The useful question is simpler: are you choosing when to use the tool? Keep the parts of technology that help you, and give the automatic parts clearer boundaries.

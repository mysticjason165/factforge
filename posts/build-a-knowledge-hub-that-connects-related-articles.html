Build a Knowledge Hub That Connects Related Articles

FactForge · 1 min read

A growing blog can become a pile of posts unless someone gives it a structure. A knowledge hub is a useful overview page that explains a subject and points readers toward more specific questions.

Choose a theme such as managing distractions. Group related articles into sensible paths: understanding interruptions, changing the environment, and planning work sessions. Write short descriptions that tell readers why each link matters. Do not duplicate entire articles on the overview page.

Review the hub when new posts appear, and remove links that no longer serve its purpose. Make sure a beginner can understand the labels without reading everything first. This approach gives your archive an entry point. It is especially useful when your best material was published at different times and would otherwise be buried behind a reverse-chronological list.

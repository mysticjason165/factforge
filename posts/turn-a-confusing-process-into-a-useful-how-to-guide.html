Turn a Confusing Process into a Useful How-To Guide

FactForge · 1 min read

Technical writing begins with a reader who needs to accomplish something. That reader may not know the vocabulary you use every day. Start with the task rather than an explanation of every feature in the system.

Write the steps in the order they happen. State any requirements before the reader needs them, and place a warning beside the relevant action rather than at the end. Use a simple example to show what a successful result looks like. Screenshots should clarify a step, not replace missing instructions.

Test the guide with someone unfamiliar with the process. If they hesitate, ask what information they expected to see. Update the document when the process changes and show when it was last reviewed. A good guide reduces guesswork. That is a more useful measure of quality than how technical or impressive its sentences sound.

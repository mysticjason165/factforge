Use a Fitness Tracker as Information, Not an Order

FactForge · 1 min read

A wearable can make activity easier to record, but a colorful score can look more authoritative than it is. Check the device's intended purpose and the meaning of each metric before using it to make decisions.

Choose one practical use, such as remembering a walk or keeping a record of sessions. Compare the number with what actually happened rather than assuming the software has a complete picture. General wellness information is different from a medical diagnosis; the FDA makes that distinction in its guidance on low-risk wellness devices.

Do not push through concerning symptoms because an app says you are ready, or assume you are unwell solely because a score changed. Seek appropriate advice when health concerns arise. Keep the tool only as complicated as it needs to be. Its job is to support your judgment, not replace every signal from your own experience.

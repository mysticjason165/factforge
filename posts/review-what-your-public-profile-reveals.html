Review What Your Public Profile Reveals

FactForge · 1 min read

A public profile can reveal more than its owner intended when separate details are combined. Review it as a stranger would, without relying on what you know is hidden in your account settings.

Look at the bio, old posts, visible contact details, and photographs that show personal information. Decide what needs to be public for the purpose of the account. A portfolio may need a contact method without needing your private home address or daily routine.

Change what you can through the platform's official controls and check the result when signed out if possible. Remember that removing a post does not guarantee every copy disappears. The exercise is about reducing unnecessary exposure, not achieving total invisibility. Repeat it when the account's purpose changes, because information that suited an old use may no longer belong in a public-facing project.

Make One Specific Plan to Reconnect

FactForge · 1 min read

Wanting more connection can remain vague for a long time. A specific invitation is easier to act on than a general promise to catch up. Think of one person you would like to hear from and one modest activity you could suggest.

Offer a time or a choice of times without making a refusal feel like a test of the relationship. A short call, a shared lunch, or a walk may fit better than a large event. Ask a real question and leave room to hear the answer.

If the invitation does not work, try another time or another opportunity rather than treating one response as a complete judgment. Relationships have different rhythms and constraints. You do not need to transform your entire social life in a week. Begin with a manageable act of contact that respects both your interest and the other person's availability.

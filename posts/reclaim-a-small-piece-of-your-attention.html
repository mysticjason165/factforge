Reclaim a Small Piece of Your Attention

FactForge · 1 min read

A scattered afternoon does not have to become a verdict on your character. Sometimes the task is unclear, the phone is within reach, and every open tab suggests another direction. Start by changing the conditions around one work session.

Choose a task with a visible finish: outline one section, read three pages, or answer one important message. Put unrelated material away and leave a note describing what you will do next. The focus manuscript's central idea is useful here: attention deserves deliberate protection rather than endless demands to try harder.

When you drift, return to the next step without turning the interruption into a long argument with yourself. Afterward, note what pulled you away. A small, repeatable session gives you information about your working conditions. It is a practical experiment, not a promise that a timer will solve every concentration difficulty.

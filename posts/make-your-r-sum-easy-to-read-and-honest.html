Make Your Résumé Easy to Read and Honest

FactForge · 1 min read

A résumé should help a reader connect your experience with the role. Unclear labels and exaggerated claims make that harder. Start with the job's actual requirements, then choose examples that show relevant work, study, or volunteer experience.

Use straightforward headings and describe what you did. If you improved a process, explain the change without inventing a percentage. Include tools you can genuinely use and projects you can discuss in an interview. Save the file in the format requested by the employer rather than assuming one layout works everywhere.

Check that the text can be selected and read in the exported document. Ask another person whether the most relevant information is easy to find. No template guarantees passage through hiring software. The goal is a truthful, readable record that gives a recruiter a reason to ask you more specific questions.

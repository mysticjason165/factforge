Turn One Original Idea into Several Digital Products

FactForge · 1 min read

A useful idea can take more than one form. A study-planning method might become a printable worksheet, an editable spreadsheet, and a short instruction guide. Those products serve different preferences while sharing the same underlying work.

Start with the version that solves the problem most clearly. Test whether another person can use it without asking you to explain every step. Only then consider additional formats. A spreadsheet needs working formulas; a printable needs enough writing space; a guide needs examples. Changing the file extension is not the same as adapting the experience.

Keep a master copy and a simple revision log so corrections reach every version. Use material and assets you have permission to distribute. Multiple products can increase the work of support and maintenance, so expand when the extra format has a clear purpose—not merely because it creates another listing.

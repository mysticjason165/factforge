Adapt a Podcast for Another Language with Care

FactForge · 1 min read

Translating a podcast involves more than swapping words. Jokes, examples, references, and pacing can change meaning across languages. A readable transcript is a useful starting point because it makes those choices easier to inspect.

Choose a short segment and have a fluent reviewer examine the translation in context. Mark names and technical terms that need consistent treatment. If you use a synthetic voice, make sure you have the necessary permission and do not imply that a person recorded speech they never approved.

Listen to the finished version while following the text. Check whether pauses and emphasis support the intended meaning. Keep a record of revisions and identify the language version clearly. Begin with a manageable amount of material rather than converting an entire archive at once. Quality depends on what listeners understand, not simply on how quickly another audio file appears.

Make Mobility Practice Comfortable and Specific

FactForge · 1 min read

Mobility is more useful as a practical question than a demand to become flexible everywhere. Think about a movement you want to perform more comfortably, such as reaching, turning, or getting into a position needed for an activity.

Choose gentle, suitable practice and avoid forcing a range because someone else can demonstrate it. A qualified professional can help when pain, injury, or a medical condition affects movement. A social-media stretch cannot determine what is causing a limitation.

Notice how the practice fits into your day and whether it remains comfortable. Do not judge progress only by an extreme pose or a dramatic before-and-after image. The purpose is usable movement, not winning a flexibility comparison. If a movement repeatedly hurts, change course and seek appropriate advice rather than assuming repeated discomfort is the price of improvement.

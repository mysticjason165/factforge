Make 3D Assets That Other People Can Actually Use

FactForge · 1 min read

A digital model can look beautiful in a preview and still be frustrating to use. Buyers may need clean geometry, understandable file names, sensible scale, and files that open in their software. Presentation and usability are separate parts of the product.

Choose a narrow collection, such as simple desk objects or reusable room components. Test the files in the formats you intend to provide. Include a short guide that explains scale, included textures, and any known limitations. Use your own designs or properly licensed source material.

Ask someone to import an asset without your help. Their questions can reveal missing instructions that you no longer notice. Avoid promising compatibility you have not checked. A smaller collection with clear documentation is a better starting point than a huge pack that leaves users guessing how its pieces fit together.

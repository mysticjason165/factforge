Treat a Career Idea as a Small Experiment

FactForge · 1 min read

A career question can feel overwhelming when the only imagined options are commit completely or do nothing. A small experiment can provide information without pretending that you already know how the whole path will unfold.

Choose a bounded way to experience the work. Complete a sample project, speak with someone doing the role, or try an introductory task using appropriate resources. Decide what you want to learn: whether you enjoy the activity, what skills it requires, or what constraints it creates.

Set limits on time and expense before starting. Review the experience honestly, including the parts that were less appealing. One experiment cannot settle an entire career decision, but it can improve the next question. You are allowed to investigate an interest without immediately turning it into a permanent identity or abandoning your existing responsibilities.

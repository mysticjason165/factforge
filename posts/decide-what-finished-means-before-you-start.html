Decide What Finished Means Before You Start

FactForge · 1 min read

Perfection can become a moving target when a task has no clear stopping point. You finish a draft, notice a possible improvement, and begin another round without knowing what would count as enough.

Before starting, list the requirements that matter. A blog post might need an accurate answer, readable structure, working links, and a careful edit. Decorative changes can wait until those are met. Distinguish a real error from a preference you could keep changing indefinitely.

Set aside one review pass and decide what you will inspect during it. When the requirements are satisfied, finish the version and record ideas for later. This does not mean accepting careless work. It means giving quality a definition you can actually use. A finished, reviewable piece teaches you more about the next improvement than an endlessly revised file nobody sees.

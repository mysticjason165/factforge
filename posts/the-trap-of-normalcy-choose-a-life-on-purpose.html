The Trap of Normalcy: Choose a Life on Purpose

FactForge · 1 min read

A familiar path can be useful without being right for every person. The problem begins when you follow it without examining whether it fits your values and responsibilities. Being ordinary is not a failure; living entirely by unexamined expectations is worth questioning.

Choose one decision that feels automatic. Ask where the expectation came from and what you would choose if you had to explain the reason in your own words. Consider the costs of changing course as seriously as the appeal of something different.

You do not need to reject college, work, or a conventional goal to live deliberately. You may choose the same path with a clearer understanding of why. The focus manuscript's emphasis on intentional attention fits here: give your own decisions enough space to be examined before the surrounding noise decides them for you.

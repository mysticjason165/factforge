Offer Document Formatting with a Defined Scope

FactForge · 1 min read

Document formatting can involve headings, page numbers, tables, spacing, and consistency. It is different from rewriting the content or verifying every claim. A clear service description prevents those tasks from getting mixed together.

Ask for the intended format and a sample before estimating the work. Explain whether the offer includes editing text, checking references, or only adjusting layout. Work on a copy and keep the original intact. After making changes, inspect the exported result because a document that looks fine in one editor may shift elsewhere.

Provide a short handoff note identifying the files and any unresolved issues. For school assignments, preserve the student's authorship and follow the relevant rules. A reliable formatting service is built on careful review and clear boundaries, not on promising to make every document perfect regardless of its condition or deadline.

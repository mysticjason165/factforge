Check Your Understanding Before Rereading Everything

FactForge · 1 min read

Familiarity with a page can feel like understanding. Close the material briefly and try to explain its main idea in your own words. That gives you a practical way to see which parts remain unclear.

Choose a small section rather than an entire chapter. Write the question it answers, the answer, and one example. Then compare your explanation with the source. Correct the missing or inaccurate parts without treating the exercise as a grade.

Use the result to guide the next study session. If the definition is clear but the application is not, practice an example instead of copying the definition again. This is a simple way to make studying more specific. The goal is not to prove you remember every sentence; it is to find out what you can actually explain and use without the page doing the work for you.

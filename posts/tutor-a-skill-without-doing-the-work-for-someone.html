Tutor a Skill Without Doing the Work for Someone

FactForge · 1 min read

Tutoring should help a learner understand and practice. If you take over the assignment, the finished answer can hide the fact that the learner still cannot perform the task. Begin by asking them to explain where they became stuck.

Use a similar example rather than solving their graded problem outright. Demonstrate a step, ask them to try another, and invite them to explain the reasoning. If you do not know an answer, check it instead of improvising confidently. Stay within the subject and level you can teach well.

End with a small independent exercise and discuss what to practice next. Be clear about session length, cost, and cancellation expectations before beginning a paid arrangement. A productive session gives the learner more control over the next problem. It does not make them dependent on you to finish each new assignment.

Prepare Meal Components Instead of an Entire Perfect Week

FactForge · 1 min read

Meal preparation can feel less demanding when you prepare a few useful components rather than every breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Think about the moments when having something ready would make the biggest difference.

Choose a small set of foods you already know how to handle and store safely. You might prepare ingredients for a bowl or wash produce for the next day. Keep food-safety instructions in mind, including refrigeration and storage times, instead of assuming every prepared meal lasts the same length of time.

Leave flexibility for changing plans and preferences. A system that creates unwanted leftovers may need to be smaller, not more disciplined. Note which prepared items you actually used before making the next list. The purpose is to reduce a practical barrier to eating, not to produce a photograph of identical containers that does not match your week.

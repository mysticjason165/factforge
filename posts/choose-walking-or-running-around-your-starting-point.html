Choose Walking or Running Around Your Starting Point

FactForge · 1 min read

Walking and running are activities, not competing identities. A choice between them should consider comfort, ability, time, and preference. You do not need to begin with the more demanding option to make activity worthwhile.

The CDC explains that intensity is relative to effort. A brisk walk may be meaningful activity for one person, while another person prefers running. Start where you can move appropriately and progress gradually if that suits your goals. Do not use pain as a sign that you need to try harder.

For weight-related goals, avoid assuming a particular activity guarantees a specific result. Many factors affect the outcome. Choose a routine you can realistically continue and ask for individual guidance when needed. A walk you can repeat comfortably may serve you better than a running plan that leaves you dreading the next session or ignoring an injury.

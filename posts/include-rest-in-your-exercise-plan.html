Include Rest in Your Exercise Plan

FactForge · 1 min read

An exercise schedule describes only part of the week. Sleep, meals, ordinary responsibilities, and recovery time also need room. Adding more sessions without looking at the whole schedule can create a plan that is difficult to maintain.

The NHLBI recommends seven to nine hours of sleep for adults. Think about whether an early or late workout repeatedly cuts into the opportunity to rest. You may need to change the session time, shorten optional activities, or reconsider how much you are trying to fit in.

Do not treat a missed workout as a reason to sacrifice more sleep the next day. Look for a routine that supports both activity and rest. Persistent exhaustion or other concerning symptoms deserve individual attention. A sustainable plan accounts for the person doing the exercise, not just the number of sessions written on the calendar.
